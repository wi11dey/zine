The FLF were created by various authors including myself and Mike Wright. When
Casady & Greene, Inc. closed the rights went back to the authors. I created
ChicagoFLF and CheckboxFLF. You are free to use them as you wish. I consider
those two in the public domain.

Regards,
Robin Casady
